Academic Session Roll-Over

This tool automates the creation of the new academic year structure while preserving all historical data.
Batch Archiving

Current batches stay intact. Students remain members of these batches so their past attendance and marks are preserved.

Automated Migration

New batches are created for the next grade level. Students are automatically enrolled in the corresponding next-standard batch.

Important Notice
  • Current 12th standard students will be deactivated (marked as Alumni).
  • 9th to 11th standard students will have their grade levels incremented.
  • All Subject-Teacher mappings are cloned to the new batches.
